TuneCamp Iris — Air-Gapped Optical Transfer

Send a file between two devices using nothing but a screen and a camera. Open send on the device with the file (e.g. your offline studio laptop) and receive on your smartphone, then point the camera at the flickering QR stream.

No network path between the devices is used — the payload travels as light. Perfect for securely transferring Zen Identity Passports or small audio snippets.
